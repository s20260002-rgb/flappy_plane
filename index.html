The product

Five seconds to learn. Just one more run to master.

Flappy Plane is a small, carefully tuned arcade game about rhythm and restraint. Every tap changes your altitude. Every gap asks for a calmer hand.

Why it feels different

Small game. Considered details.

Designed to be understood at a glance, then enjoyed for one more attempt.

01

One-button flight

Tap, click or press Space to boost. That's the whole control scheme.

02

A living skyline

Glass towers, drifting clouds and a parallax city keep every run in motion.

03

Medals worth chasing

Bronze, silver, gold and platinum tiers give every run a next goal.

04

Sound you can switch off

Engine hum, score chimes and building explosions, all generated in the browser.

05

Your best, kept local

Scores stay in your browser. No account, no upload, no fuss.

06

Made for both languages

Switch between English and Traditional Chinese at any time, even mid-flight.

Flight manual

Three taps to take off.

The rules fit on a boarding pass. The mastery takes a little longer.

1

Tap to climb

Every tap gives the jet a short burst of lift. Let go and gravity does the rest.

2

Find the gap

Line up with the opening between towers. The higher your score, the tighter the fit.

3

Chase the medal

Reach 3, 10, 20 or 35 points to earn a bronze, silver, gold or platinum medal.

Under the hood

One file. Zero dependencies.

The entire experience — artwork, physics, audio, localisation and interface — lives in a single HTML file. No framework, no build step, no tracking.

  • Canvas 2D rendering with a hand-drawn jet and skyline
  • Web Audio engine sounds and generated effects
  • Local best-score storage; no account required
  • Fully responsive, keyboard and touch friendly

FAQ

Before you board.

Is Flappy Plane free?

Yes. The full game is free to play in your browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs directly on this page.

Where is my best score saved?

Locally in your browser. It never leaves your device. Clearing site data resets it.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Tap to boost, use the pause button to take a break.

Who made it?

1C Paco Au Yeung and 1C Max Chan.

Why a Boeing 303?

It's a playful, fictional livery. The 303 twinjet is hand-drawn for the game and isn't a real production aircraft.
